# Dunsin Natural Writing — examples and calibration

These examples are original writing samples showing the target style. They are not quotations from Turnitout and do not imply endorsements or election results.

## Example 1 — Meeting confirmation
**Draft:** "Hi Ann-Marie, Trust your day went well? Just wanted to touch base prior to the meeting request we discussed about yesterday. Please confirm this address is fine by you."
**Refined:** "Hi Ann-Marie, I hope your day went well. Just following up on the meeting we discussed yesterday. Does Starbucks at 456 Welland Avenue work for you? Thanks, Dunsin."
**Why:** Keeps the warmth, removes clutter, and makes the question easy to answer.

## Example 2 — Resident concern
**Input:** "I'm fed up with complaints not getting answered. Why should we believe council will listen?"
**Refined reply:** "I understand why you're frustrated. When residents take the time to raise concerns, they deserve a response and a clear explanation of what happens next. That's the kind of accountability I want to bring to Ward 5. You should be able to reach your councillor, get a straight answer, and see whether follow-up actually happens."
**Why:** Names the actual issue without inventing a fix or promising authority not yet held.

## Example 3 — Public campaign invitation
**Refined caption:** "Ward 5, what's one thing you want your next councillor to understand? Not a slogan. Not a rehearsed answer. A real concern or idea from your neighbourhood. I'm listening, and I'm happy to have a constructive conversation. Call/Text: 647-498-8836."
**Why:** One theme, a human rhythm, and an accessible invitation.

## Example 4 — Firm but respectful
**Draft:** "It seems every time you contact me it is to ask for money and I don't think that is fair."
**Refined:** "I've noticed that most of our recent conversations have been about financial requests, and it's making me uncomfortable. I'd rather we have a connection that isn't centred on money. I won't be able to help financially, and I hope you'll respect that."
**Why:** Clear boundary without humiliation.

## Example 5 — Short social video script
**Refined:** "Ward 5, I have a question. When was the last time you raised a concern and actually heard back? We talk a lot about big ideas in an election. But being available and accountable matters, too. Tell me what needs attention in your neighbourhood. I'm listening."
**Why:** Speakable, grounded, no grand promises.

## Example 6 — Professional email
**Refined:** "Subject: Follow-up on our discussion\n\nHello,\n\nI'm following up on our conversation and wanted to confirm the next step. Could you let me know whether the proposed arrangement works for you? If you'd prefer another option, I'm open to discussing it.\n\nRegards,\nDunsin Fakorede"
**Why:** Direct reason, one clear question, calm professionalism.

## Editing checklist
1. Can the first sentence be deleted without losing content? If so, delete it.
2. Does each paragraph answer a question the reader might actually have?
3. Are there any unverifiable facts, assurances, or promises?
4. Could the recipient reasonably read this as personal and sincere?
5. If told to be brief, did you produce only the needed text?
